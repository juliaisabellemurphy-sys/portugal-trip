Benagil Kayak Tour + Beach Hopping

Move to Lagos today.

Booked

You'll see: Benagil Cave, sea arches, hidden beaches, and coastal caves. Also near Praia do Carvalho (beach), reached through a cliff tunnel entrance.

Late Morning

Visit the Seven Hanging Valleys Trail and hit Praia da Marinha beach — a small amount of rentals; arrive early.

Praia da Marinha

Often considered the most beautiful beach in Portugal. Don't miss: the heart-shaped rock viewpoint, swimming, and the cliff-top views.

Sunset

Ponta da Piedade — one of the most spectacular sunset spots in Portugal.
