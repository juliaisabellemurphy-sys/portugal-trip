Day 3 — Sunday, Sept 6

Sintra Day Trip

Leave Lisbon around 8:00 AM.

Getting there: Go to Rossio Station and take the direct CP train to Sintra — about 40 minutes, running roughly every 20 minutes. Tickets run €2–3 each way, purchased at the station. From Sintra Station, Bus 434 serves the historic center, Moorish Castle, and Pena Palace. Uber/Bolt or a taxi also works, though traffic can build later in the morning.

For Any Evening in Town

Lisbon Cocktail Shortlist

The can't-miss list — ten bars worth working into your evenings, ranked.

1

Red Frog

The Big One

Serious, inventive cocktails with a speakeasy atmosphere. Hidden entrance, intimate room, excellent technique — consistently ranked among Lisbon's best. Book ahead if possible.

2

Quattro Teste

Best Insider Pick

Creative cocktails without the pretension. Small and inventive — the Basque burnt-cheesecake cocktail is a signature people rave about. Great if you like bartenders who talk you through the menu.

3

Foxtrot

Best Atmosphere

Old-school Lisbon in a gorgeous, dark Art Deco-ish space. More about atmosphere and properly made classics than experimental mixology — a Lisbon institution dating back decades.

4

Pavilhão Chinês

The Most Fun

A cabinet-of-curiosities setting with antique toys, collections, and multiple rooms. Cocktails are good, but the experience is the star.

5

QG Atelier Bar

Hidden Gem

Tiny, intimate, bartender-driven, with a focus on classics and customized drinks — worth choosing over a trendy tourist bar.

6

Imprensa Cocktail and Oyster Bar

Cocktails + Oysters

A sophisticated early-evening stop — cocktails plus something salty to eat. Small, atmospheric, particularly good for a couple.

7

Catfish Cocktail Bar

Current Local Favorite

Creative drinks and a relaxed local feel — recognized as Bar of the Year in the 2026 Prémios Mesa Marcada awards cycle.

8

The Royal Vessel Bar

Sexy Late-Night Option

Intimate, dark, old-world atmosphere with a maritime theme, good cocktails, and a nice late-night vibe.

9

Procópio

Old Lisbon

Open since 1972, with an old-world Art Nouveau interior — history and classic cocktails; the bar itself feels like part of Lisbon's history.

10

Bar A Paródia

The Deep-Cut

A genuinely old-school, slightly secretive experience dating to 1974, with classic cocktails and an eccentric atmosphere — less polished than Red Frog, but arguably more characterful.

If you only have time for 3: Red Frog → Quattro Teste → Foxtrot.
If atmosphere matters more than technique: swap Quattro Teste for Pavilhão Chinês.
